# Printers

## Adding an office printer
1. Open Settings > Bluetooth & devices > Printers & scanners.
2. Click "Add device", then "Add manually", and enter the printer path \\printserver01\<printer-name>.
3. Printer names are printed on the label on the front of each device, for example FLOOR2-COLOR.

## Common printer issues
- Print job stuck in the queue: open the queue, cancel all documents, and restart the Print Spooler service.
- Paper jam or toner low: contact Facilities on extension 2200; IT does not replace toner.
- "Driver unavailable": remove the printer and add it again so the driver reinstalls from the print server.

## Secure printing
- Confidential documents should be sent to the "SecurePrint" queue and released at any printer by tapping your ID badge.

# Password Reset and Account Lockout

## Self-service password reset
1. Go to the self-service portal at https://passwordreset.company.local.
2. Enter your corporate email address and complete the MFA challenge (Authenticator app or SMS).
3. Choose a new password that meets the policy below, then sign out of all devices and sign in again.

## Password policy
- Minimum 12 characters, with at least one uppercase letter, one number and one symbol.
- The last 5 passwords cannot be reused.
- Passwords expire every 90 days. You receive an email reminder 14 days before expiry.

## Account lockout
- An account locks automatically after 5 failed sign-in attempts.
- The lock is released automatically after 30 minutes.
- For an urgent unlock, call the Service Desk on extension 4357 (HELP) and verify your identity with your employee ID.

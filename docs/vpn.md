# VPN Access (GlobalProtect)

## Installing the VPN client
1. Open the Software Center (Windows) or Self Service (macOS).
2. Search for "GlobalProtect" and click Install.
3. When prompted for a portal address, enter vpn.company.local.

## Connecting
- Sign in with your corporate email and password, then approve the MFA push notification.
- VPN is required to reach internal systems such as the intranet, file shares and the ERP.

## Troubleshooting
- "Gateway not responding": check your internet connection, then disconnect and reconnect.
- Error "Authentication failed": your password may have expired; reset it through the self-service portal first.
- Slow VPN: close large downloads and streaming, and switch from Wi-Fi to a wired connection if possible.
- If the problem persists, raise a ticket in the IT portal under category "Network > VPN" with a screenshot of the error.
